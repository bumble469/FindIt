  2. 02

  4. 04

Quick guide

What should I do?

Whether you lost something or picked something up, here is the short version.

Lost something?

Get it back

  1. 1

    Search first. Browse items. Someone may have already handed it in.

  2. 2

    Report it. Add where and when you lost it, plus a clear description and photo if you have one.

  3. 3

    Track it. Watch My Items for a match, then verify at the campus office. Bring a photo ID.

🔒 Unique identifying details you add are never shown publicly. Keep them for verification.

Found something?

Return it

  1. 1

    Report it. Say where you found it and add a photo so the owner can recognise it.

  2. 2

    Choose where it is. Keep it safe with you, or deposit it at the campus office.

  3. 3

    Hand it over. When a claim arrives, check it with the office, then mark the item as returned.

📦 Can't reach the office today? Report it now and mark that you're holding it.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

Anyone can browse items. You need a free account to report an item, claim one, and follow your reports in My Items.

How do I claim an item that is mine?

Open the item from Browse Items and press Claim. The item moves to "Claim pending", and you confirm ownership at the campus office with a photo ID. Once it's handed over, the report is marked as returned.

My item isn't listed. What now?

Report it as lost with as much detail as you can. If someone later reports a match, the status in My Items changes to "Match found".

I found something but can't get to the office today.

Report it anyway and choose "I have it with me". When you've dropped it at the office, press "Handed to office" in My Items so owners know where to go.

Are my identifying details visible to everyone?

No. The unique identifying details you add when reporting a lost item are not shown publicly. They stay available for verification.

How long are items kept?

Items are held at the SPIT Ground Floor Lost & Found Office for 30 days. Please contact the office for details about unclaimed items.
